01 / Executive Summary

The plan in one page.

Construction is the largest industry on earth and the least digitized. As AI copilots, sensors, and autonomous machines arrive on job sites, no one is coordinating them against the plan, and no one is producing a trusted record of what actually happened. AROBOTIX is building that missing layer.

What we do

AROBOTIX is a neutral coordination and verification layer that sits between the digital construction model (BIM) and the job site. It reads the plan, breaks it into structured tasks, sequences work across humans and machines, and produces a confidence-tagged record of execution called the Sequence-Validated Execution Record (SVER). It supports the contractor's authority. It does not replace it.

Why it matters

Every other complex industry, from logistics to manufacturing, runs on a system that reads intent, routes work to people and machines, closes the loop, and learns. Construction still runs on PDFs, phone calls, and tribal knowledge. That gap costs the industry hundreds of billions of dollars a year in delays, rework, and disputes.

Where we are

Proof of Concept complete and in internal testing. A non-binding MOU is in place with Hoa Binh Construction Group, Vietnam's largest general contractor, for a structured 90-day pilot targeted for Q3 2026. Core IP filings are in progress. Confirmed industry advisor: Jim Bedrick, FAIA, co-author of the BIMForum LOD Specification.

The ask

We are raising a $700K pre-seed round on a SAFE at a $6M post-money cap to complete the BIM-to-Task Compiler and the Construction Task Object Model, file core patents, and run the Hoa Binh pilot. A seed round of $2.5M to $4M is planned for 2027 once pilot results are in hand.

03 / The Problem

The plan is perfect. The execution is not.

The world spends roughly $16 trillion a year building things. Most projects run over budget and behind schedule, not because the plans were wrong, but because no one can reliably coordinate and verify what happens on site.

The plans exist

Architects and engineers model every building in detail before a single hole is dug.

The people exist

Skilled crews and contractors are ready, experienced, and accountable.

The robots are arriving

Automation is showing up on job sites worldwide, and the trend only accelerates.

Between the perfect digital plan and the real site lies a gap. No one decides which machine does what, when, and in what order alongside the others. There is no trusted record that the work happened. When errors and disputes occur, there is no reliable trail to follow. Robots are the unpredictable physical element that needs coordination, sequencing, and verification.

06 / Product & Technology

The platforms, in the order they must be built.

AROBOTIX is an AI-native system with engineered guardrails. The wedge is a compiler that turns models into structured, verifiable work. Everything else is built on top of it.

01 · Foundational wedge

BIM-to-Executable Task Compiler

Reads the digital model and compiles it into structured execution tasks with readiness validation, sequencing, and dependency mapping. In the Crawl phase, classification is rule-based, which is sufficient and more defensible for the pilot than a black-box model. Built before any UI work.

02 · Architecture

Construction Task Object Model (CTOM)

The schema everything depends on, locked first. Organized by execution pattern, not trade name, across ten field groups, each with its own readiness prerequisites, verification requirements, and dependencies. This is the difference between a system that compounds and one that breaks on project two.

03 · Hero output

Sequence-Validated Execution Record (SVER)

A confidence-tagged record showing that a task was executed under validated readiness conditions, in its correct position within the model-derived dependency chain, with an attached evidence trail. It is a defensive record and a dispute-reduction layer, not automated QA signoff, legal certification, or fault assignment. Human review and final accountability stay central.

04 · The moat

Execution Data Graph

Every project produces a dataset no competitor can replicate: the first verified record of how construction actually happens at task level. It compounds with every build and powers benchmarking, risk prediction, and smarter coordination.

08 / Competition & Moat

Three layers. Only one is unclaimed.

The design layer is owned and consolidating. The worker and machine layer is the most heavily funded part of construction technology right now. The layer between them, where the model becomes sequenced, readiness-validated work and the execution record is held, has no incumbent. AROBOTIX claims layer 2.

Layer 1 · Design

Autodesk, Trimble, Bentley, Nemetschek

Owns the model and the documentation around it. Stops at design intent. These are upstream inputs to AROBOTIX, not rivals for the same buyer decision. Both Autodesk and Trimble spent early 2026 acquiring rather than building at the edges of their stack, which is the behavior of platforms defending a core.

Layer 2 · Coordination

Unclaimed

Converts the model into sequenced, dependency-mapped, readiness-validated tasks and holds the execution record. Pieces of this are attacked from both sides. Nobody holds the whole chain. This is the position AROBOTIX is built to occupy.

Layer 3 · Work

Copilots, layout robots, autonomous machines

Coaches, inspects, prints, and moves at the point of work. Dusty Robotics prints BIM layout onto the slab. Built Robotics and Boston Dynamics automate machines and inspection rounds. A new class of worker-level AI copilots answers questions at the workface. All of these are evidence sources into layer 2, not competitors for it.

What stops a well-funded neighbor from taking layer 2

The honest answer comes first. Nothing structural stops ALICE from extending downward from the schedule into the execution record, Buildots or Doxel from moving upstream from observation into compilation, or a well-capitalized worker-layer copilot from building a task compiler. Several of these companies have raised more in a single round than AROBOTIX plans to raise across its first two. They have the capital, the headcount, and the general contractor relationships. AROBOTIX has none of those advantages and will not claim otherwise. What follows is not a claim of impossibility. It is a cost argument and a role argument.

What AROBOTIX does hold is a live pilot with a top-five Vietnamese general contractor beginning Q4 2026, inside a 25-year contractor and government network that no capital-rich entrant can buy on a two-year timeline. It also sits on the BIMForum DfMA Working Group and the Vietnam BIM Steering Committee, which means the readiness and sequencing definitions AROBOTIX builds against are the same ones being drafted into industry standards.

Cost to copy

Task decomposition from IFC requires model-level reading, DfMA sequencing logic, cross-trade dependency mapping, and ISO 19650 fluency. That is a domain hire and a multi-year build, not a feature sprint. It buys time. It does not buy safety, and it decays the moment a competitor hires the expertise.

Role conflict, the permanent one

If a vendor owns the work and the record of the work, that vendor writes the test and grades the test. A general contractor or owner in a dispute cannot lean on a record produced by the same company whose product performed the task being measured. This applies to every layer 3 player and to any layer 1 platform with a product line to protect. AROBOTIX can never be that conflicted, because AROBOTIX never touches the work. That is not a head start. It is a role they cannot occupy without breaking their own model.

Data moat

Features can be copied. Operational intelligence compounds. The Execution Data Graph only exists because AROBOTIX ran the projects to create it, and gets harder to replicate with every build.

Human-first, so it sticks

The system is built around contractor authority, not against it. The reason general contractors will trust it is the same reason they cannot easily rip it out.

AROBOTIX runs on sites with no AI copilots, no glasses, and no robots. Where those exist, their output becomes evidence in the record. Where they do not, the record is unchanged.
